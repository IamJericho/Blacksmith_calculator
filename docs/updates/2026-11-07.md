## Starfall discounts — deployment #447

One group per order: Staff 20%, Young Guard 10%, Supporter 5%. Applies to the overall total, including delivery and reinforcement, rounded up to whole Tek. The order records the selected Starfall group. Website and Discord imports preserve it.

## Equipment blueprint updates — deployment #448

26 library records updated: Hazmat, Arctic, Fur and Flak (five pieces each), Gas Mask, Glider Suit and four Scuba pieces. Item Rating and Durability use full labels. See the catalog reference for exact values.

Gas Mask shared recipe: Crystal 43, Absorbent Substrate 8, Polymer 87; Durability 152. Its customer selling price remains 15 Tek. Other equipment changes in this batch were blueprint metadata, not recipe changes.

## Analytics and processing — deployments #449–450

- Material and Item Analytics: Weekly, Monthly, four calendar quarters with year selection, rolling 1 year and 3 year. Weeks start Sunday and end Saturday.
- Processing Packing List totals include saved crafting progress across all processing orders. Shared-item adjustments identify the customer; no guessed allocation between customers. Individual and combined lists update together.

## Account and activity interface — deployments #451–452

- Access history moved from Profile to Activity Log; Access and Activity filters added.
- Loaded order edits are grouped into expandable order entries, with individual undo controls. Older activity can be loaded to include earlier edits.
- Access events grouped into collapsed account sections.
- Owner Profile lists accounts and exposes existing password-reset controls; temporary passwords require 12+ characters and invalidate existing sessions.
