# Blacksmith pricing update — November 8, 2026

Removed the 1,000 Tek delivery surcharge from each standard full-dedi material rate. New recipe-based quotes use the lower rates, with the usual selling-price markup and rounding. For example, the standard full-dedi rates for Hide, Fiber and Metal Ingots went from 1,400, 1,500 and 4,000 Tek to 400, 500 and 3,000 Tek.

| Item | Previous selling price (Tek) | New selling price (Tek) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rex | 35 | 20 |
| Carcharodontosaurus | 55 | 30 |
| Archelon | 100 | 40 |

Of 205 catalog entries checked, 174 sell for less and 31 retain their prices because they use fixed pricing or round to the same amount. The separate delivery charge for finished orders still applies. Accepted quotes retain their recorded payment. Resource payment exchange rates that derive from standard dedi values use the updated rates.

See [the catalog reference](../CATALOG-REFERENCE.md) for the full set of base selling prices.
